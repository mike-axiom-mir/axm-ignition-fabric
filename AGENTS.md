# AGENTS.md

## Lane rule

One AI chat/instance gets exactly one lane.

- Do not spread one chat across multiple lanes.
- Keep all work for that chat inside its claimed lane.
- Do not claim additional lanes from the same chat.
- Do not silently move work into another lane.
- A lane change requires an explicit reassignment.
- Preserve lane receipts, issue/PR references, and handoff context so another chat can take a different lane cleanly.

Short form:

> 1 AI CHAT = 1 LANE. NO SPREAD.
